Renamed in 0.8 ​

TensND 0.8 names its functions as Julia does: lowercase, a proper name included, as cholesky or schur in LinearAlgebra; types keep their capitals. The former names still work and warn once, naming their successor; they will be removed in a later minor release. The moduli keep their symbols as exact aliases, without a warning.
