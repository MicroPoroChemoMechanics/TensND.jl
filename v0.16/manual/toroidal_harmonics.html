Toroidal functions ​

See also

Theory: Toroidal coordinates · Toroidal functions · Curvilinear differential calculus

Manual: Coordinate systems (symbolic) · Charts and coordinate conversions

Tutorials: An insulating torus in a uniform flux

How to evaluate the functions on which the harmonic functions of a toroidal chart are built. The definitions and the method are on Toroidal functions.

The functions and their degrees ​

Every function returns the values for all the degrees up to N at once: the entry n + 1 is the function of degree  , at the order m and at the coordinate .

julia
using TensND

toroidal_P(3, 0, 1.0)          # P₋₁⸝₂, P₁⸝₂, P₃⸝₂, P₅⸝₂ at cosh α, α = 1
4-element Vector{Float64}:
 0.9408621592493499
 1.1886781524674594
 2.1320142643842144
 4.550584987753362
julia
toroidal_Q(3, 0, 1.0)
4-element Vector{Float64}:
 1.975364432288866
 0.3699506249680232
 0.10269671620498258
 0.031580527475226786

The first kind is bounded on the axis of the chart,  , where every degree equals 1, and grows toward the focal circle; the second kind is singular on the axis and decays toward the focal circle.

julia
toroidal_P(3, 0, 0.0)
4-element Vector{Float64}:
 1.0
 1.0
 1.0
 1.0

The order is the second argument:

julia
toroidal_Q(3, 2, 1.0)
4-element Vector{Float64}:
 2.498468496925912
 1.8466913714025246
 1.0939677098629998
 0.577803505804538

Derivatives ​

toroidal_dP and toroidal_dQ return the derivatives with respect to , which a condition on a torus   needs.

julia
toroidal_dQ(3, 0, 1.0)
4-element Vector{Float64}:
 -1.139462753988439
 -0.5975575904191137
 -0.26992992188505666
 -0.11480037654953673

They agree with the derivative of the values by ForwardDiff, which the functions accept:

julia
using ForwardDiff
ForwardDiff.derivative(α -> toroidal_Q(3, 0, α), 1.0)
4-element Vector{Float64}:
 -1.139462753988439
 -0.5975575904191137
 -0.26992992188505666
 -0.11480037654953676

A harmonic function of the chart ​

The product of , of a toroidal function and of a sine or cosine of and of is harmonic. Its Laplacian, computed by the pointwise toroidal system, vanishes to rounding:

julia
CS = coorsys_toroidal(PointwiseGeometry, 1.0)
n, m = 2, 1
h(ξ) = sqrt(cosh(ξ[2]) - cos(ξ[3])) * toroidal_Q(n, m, ξ[2])[n + 1] * cos(n * ξ[3]) * cos(m * ξ[1])
ξ = [0.3, 0.8, 1.1]          # (φ, α, β)
h(ξ), laplacian(h, CS)(ξ)
(0.25019811928941016, 1.1102230246251565e-16)

The constant as a series ​

Outside the axis, the constant 1 is the series of the functions of the second kind

whose terms decay as :

julia
α, β, N = 0.8, 1.1, 40
Q = toroidal_Q(N, 0, α)
sqrt(2) / π * sqrt(cosh(α) - cos(β)) * sum((n == 0 ? 1 : 2) * Q[n + 1] * cos(n * β) for n in 0:N)
1.0000000000000004

Number types ​

The functions take numbers and dual numbers. They are computed by iterations that stop on a value, and refuse a symbol:

julia
using Symbolics
@variables a
try
    toroidal_Q(2, 0, a)
catch err
    println(sprint(showerror, err))
end
ArgumentError: the toroidal functions are evaluated by iterations that stop on the value of α: a number or a dual is needed, got a Symbolics.Num
