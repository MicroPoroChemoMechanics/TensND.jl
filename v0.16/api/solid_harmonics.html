TensND.lattice_solid_harmonics Function
julia
lattice_solid_harmonics(x, periods, N; α = nothing, exclude = ()) -> SolidHarmonicTable

Decaying solid harmonics of a unit source repeated on the orthogonal lattice of periods , at the point x and up to the degree N:  , with the periodic potential of the source with its uniform background and the derivative for which   (irregular_solid_harmonics). The degree 0 is itself. At a lattice point the source of that point is left out: the table holds the field its images send there.

The sums are Ewald's, which converge as a Gaussian at every degree; the result does not depend on the splitting parameter α, by default. The images listed in exclude by their integer coordinates are left out as well: their short-range terms are skipped and the long-range part they carry into the reciprocal sum is taken off, which is free of cancellation for the images within or so.

julia> S = lattice_solid_harmonics(zeros(3), (1.0, 1.0, 1.0), 0);

julia> real(S[0, 0]) ≈ -2.837297479480620
true
