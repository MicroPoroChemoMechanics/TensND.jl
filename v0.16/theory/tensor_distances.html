6. Which distance? ​

The closest isotropic tensor depends on the distance, and for the arctan- and power-Euclidean ones on the gauge. As the gauge tends to zero or to infinity, the closest isotropic tensors of the arctan-Euclidean distance and of the power-Euclidean one of exponent 1 tend to the two Euclidean ones, that of the stiffness and the inverse of that of the compliance (Morin et al., 2020); with another exponent the limits are others. The log-Euclidean and the Riemannian distances involve no gauge; the first has a closed form on every class and with symbols, the second is invariant under a wider group. The tutorial The isotropic tensor closest to a measured stiffness compares them on a measured tensor.
