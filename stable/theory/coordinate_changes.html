Changes of coordinates ​

See also

Manual: Charts and coordinate conversions

Tutorials: Ellipsoidal coordinates · Changing the focal distance of spheroidal harmonics

A coordinate system is used in two directions: from the coordinates to the point, which is the position vector, and from the point to its coordinates, which a field given in another system requires. This page writes the second direction in closed form for each predefined system, then composes the two to pass from one system to another. A chart denotes here the pair of maps, between numbers, that a system defines.
